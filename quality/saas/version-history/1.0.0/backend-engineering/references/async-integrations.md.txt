# Derived state, asynchronous effects and integration — B5

Activate only affected L15–L19 responsibilities. Inputs: state/identity/authority, consistency/freshness, operation/event schemas and replay horizons, service versions and workloads. Depend on B3 contracts and B4 atomicity; coordinate B6 admission and B7 recovery. Output working adapters and bounded, observable state machines. A durable database job is not evidence of a particular broker or workflow engine.

Keep transport request IDs distinct from logical operation IDs. | Session/stream/tool concurrency, input/output bytes, deadline, result count, auth/egress and retry scope from actual revision. | Tool metadata/output expands authority, operation replayed under new request ID, credentials redirected, unsupported negotiation or unbounded output. | Revision negotiation/schema drift, unauthorised calls/destinations, malicious results, cancel/disconnect/lost side effect and replay; absent SDK/service means runtime support unverified, not inferred from a tool description. |

Compensation is another fallible operation, not an atomic rollback of the outside world. Persist its state, authority, idempotency, retry/deadline and escalation/reconciliation; do not promise it always succeeds. A saga may use synchronous calls or messaging according to its contracts; neither transport by itself provides durable ownership or isolation. Prefer a local transaction when it satisfies the invariant with less complexity.

Failure handling: stop retry amplification, isolate/quarantine poison or uncertain work, preserve logical identity and retained context, and resume through the declared replay/repair path. Keep failures and unsupported adapter versions in the evidence record.
