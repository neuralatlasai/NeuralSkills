## Requirements, architecture and selected security mapping

Public ISO catalogues checked in this authoring session: [29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html) Edition 2, confirmed 2024 with a replacement draft under development; [42010:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html) Edition 2; [25010:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html) Edition 2; [9241-210:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html) Edition 2 confirmed 2025. Use sourced/verifiable requirements, stakeholder concerns/views/relationships and selected measurable quality objectives. Full normative ISO texts were not accessed; this is practical scope-informed traceability, not clause conformance or certification.

[OWASP ASVS 5.0.0](https://asvs.owasp.org/) is the stable 2025-05-30 release observed. Actual [V8 source](https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/blob/v5.0.0/5.0/en/0x17-V8-Authorization.md) was read. The following is a selected authoring/fixture mapping, not an ASVS level or whole-standard assessment:

| ASVS 5.0.0 ID | Selected requirement, paraphrased | Mechanism / limit / failure / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8.1.1 / 8.1.2 | Document function/data/field rules | Shared context and adapter contract; no implicit privileged fields; incomplete policy would remain unverified; inspect contracts and denial tests. |
| 8.2.1 / 8.2.2 / 8.2.3 | Restrict functions, objects and fields to granted consumers | Trusted identity and tenant/object predicates plus allowlists/projection; privileged file/admin boundary disclosed; cross-tenant/object/field integrated negatives and result paths. |
| 8.3.1 | Enforce in trusted service tier | Domain/repository/cache/job policy rather than UI; service scope explicit; direct invocation/denial tests, not browser hiding. |
| 8.4.1 | Prevent operations affecting unauthorized tenants | Tenant-inclusive identifiers/constraints and scoped keys/cursors/results; no assertion of SQLite SQL-role isolation; integrated cross-tenant tests and actual adapter coverage record. |
