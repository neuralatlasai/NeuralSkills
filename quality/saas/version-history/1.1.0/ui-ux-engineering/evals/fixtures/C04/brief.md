# Contact preferences brief

This is a text fallback for a deliberately unavailable design reference. Use a warm off-white canvas, dark charcoal text and a single dark blue action color. Prefer a narrow form, obvious labels, comfortable spacing and a clear primary action.

Heading: Stay in the loop
Description: Choose which messages you would like to receive.
Email label: Email address
Preferences: Email updates (initially checked), Product research (initially unchecked), Partner offers (initially unchecked).
Action: Save preferences
Validation: Require a syntactically valid nonblank email.
Outcome: Show a visible summary of the saved email and enabled categories. Saving is local only. Editing after saving must not silently change the saved summary until Save is activated again.
